# Code of conduct

Omark follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and Discussions, and anywhere you speak for the project.

In short:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and give and take feedback gracefully.
- Respect different views, experience and backgrounds.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private details.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Omark and for the community.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@omark.app**. Every report is read and handled in confidence by the maintainers.

Maintainers enforce the code with the steps in the Covenant: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on how serious the behaviour is and whether it is repeated.

This summary is for reading convenience; the Covenant's full text is what applies.
