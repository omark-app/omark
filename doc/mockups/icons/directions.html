Pick an Omark icon direction

Three families, each shown with the Omark Tasks and Omark Time glyphs, on light and dark, at real sizes, and with the wordmark. Colours come from the app's tokens.css. A Notes icon appears only as a greyed example of how a later sibling would look.

My pick: A, Brackets

  • A is the one only Omark could have. A task in a Markdown file is literally - [x], so the brackets carry the "own + markdown" idea, and every later app just puts a new glyph between them.
  • B is the friendliest and the boldest on a home screen, but a tick in a ring is close to many other to-do icons.
  • C says "plain files you own" most clearly, but the page and fold leave the glyph small, so it is the weakest at 16 px.

Reply in the session with a letter. Mixes are fine, for example "A, but with B's coloured tiles". After the pick, the full set is made in brand/: SVG sources, PNGs (512, 192, maskable 512, 180, 32, favicon SVG), a 500 px avatar and a 1280×640 social preview.
