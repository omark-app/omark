Omark

Small tools that keep your work in plain files you own.

Omark is a set of open source apps for people and their AI agents. Your tasks and hours stay as Markdown and CSV in your own OneDrive, Google Drive or folder. No Omark server ever stores them.

How to start

No new account. Sign in with the Microsoft or Google account you already have, or pick a folder.

Omark Tasks

  1. Open the app.
  2. Sign in with Microsoft or Google, or pick a folder.
  3. Add your first task.

Omark Time

  1. Open the app.
  2. Sign in with Google.
  3. Title a calendar event CODE; Category; what.

Install it

Both apps install like any other app and work offline.

  • iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Chrome or Edge: click the install icon in the address bar.

Where your data lives

Omark Tasks

Three plain files: tasks.md and done.md in Markdown, and config.yaml for settings.

Omark Time

CSV files, such as events-2026.csv and projects.csv. Their source is your own calendar.

Both live in your OneDrive, Google Drive or your own folder. Open them in Obsidian or Excel, or hand them to an AI agent. Leaving is just closing the app.

Principles

  • Plain files you own. Your data stays in storage you choose. No Omark server keeps it.
  • Works with your AI agent. The formats are documented, so an agent can read and write them with you.
  • No lock-in. Leaving is just closing the app. Your files stay where they are.
  • Small and simple. A calm screen and few features, added only when they fit.
  • Open source. Read the code, run your own copy, change it.

The full principles are in PHILOSOPHY.md, published with the code at launch.
