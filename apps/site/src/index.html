Omark

Small tools that keep your work in plain files you own.

Omark is a set of open source apps. Your tasks, hours and goals stay as Markdown and CSV in your own OneDrive, Google Drive or folder, where you or an AI agent you run yourself can open them. Omark itself has no AI, and no Omark server ever stores your files.

Omark Tasks

A calm task list for today, this week and later, kept in plain Markdown.

Omark Time

Plan your day in your calendar. Omark Time turns those events into hours per project, kept in CSV.

It reads events from Google Calendars you own and, only if you turn it on, colours them.

Omark Goals

How to start

No new account. Sign in with the Microsoft or Google account you already have, or pick a folder.

Omark Tasks

  1. Open the app.
  2. Sign in with Microsoft or Google, or pick a folder.
  3. Add your first task.

Omark Time

  1. Open the app.
  2. Sign in with Google.
  3. Title a calendar event CODE; Category; what.

Install it

Omark installs like any other app and works offline.

  • iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Chrome or Edge: click the install icon in the address bar.

Where your data lives

Omark Tasks

Three plain files: tasks.md and done.md in Markdown, and config.yaml for settings.

Omark Time

CSV files, such as events-2026.csv and projects.csv. Their source is your own calendar.

Omark Goals

One Markdown file, goals.md, with a picture for each area if you like.

They all live in one Omark folder in your OneDrive, Google Drive or your own folder. Open them in Obsidian or Excel, or hand them to an AI agent. Leaving is just closing the app.

Principles

  • Plain files you own. Your data stays in storage you choose. No Omark server keeps it.
  • Works with your AI agent. The formats are documented, so an agent can read and write them with you. Omark itself has no AI built in and sends nothing to AI services (privacy).
  • No lock-in. Leaving is just closing the app. Your files stay where they are.
  • Small and simple. A calm screen and few features, added only when they fit.
  • Open source. Read the code, run your own copy, change it.

The full principles are in PHILOSOPHY.md.
