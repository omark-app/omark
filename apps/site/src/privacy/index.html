Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

In short

  • We don't store your data on our servers. Omark (its apps Omark Tasks, Omark Time and Omark Goals) runs in your browser and talks directly to the storage you pick: your OneDrive, your Google Drive, or a folder on your device. Omark Time also reads your own Google Calendar, if you connect it.
  • The apps set only a few sign-in cookies, so you stay signed in. This site sets no cookies.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party scripts.
  • No AI. Omark has no AI features and never sends your data to an AI service.
  • We ask Microsoft and Google only for the access the apps need, and ask for extra access only when you turn on the feature that needs it.

Where your data lives

Your tasks, notes, hours, goals and settings are plain files in one Omark folder in the storage you pick. The app reads and writes them from your browser. No Omark server receives or keeps a copy.

Signing in

To use OneDrive, Google Drive or Google Calendar, you sign in with your Microsoft or Google account. We never see your password: you type it on Microsoft's or Google's own page.

A small sign-in helper on our host, at /api/auth/*, finishes the sign-in. It swaps the one-time sign-in code for tokens (OAuth with PKCE). It encrypts the long-lived refresh token and keeps it in a sign-in cookie in your browser. When the app needs to reach your storage, the helper uses that cookie to give your browser a short-lived access token. The helper keeps nothing: there is no database and no copy of your tokens on our side.

All of them are sent only over HTTPS, only to the app's own site, and not with requests that other sites make in the background (Secure, host-only, SameSite=Lax). They are strictly necessary for signing in, so there is no cookie banner. Signing out removes the session, last account and signed-in cookies.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts.

On your device

So the apps work offline, they keep a copy of your files (in your browser's IndexedDB) and a few settings on your device. This copy stays in your browser. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

The demo

The demo at demo.omark.app uses made-up data kept only in your browser. It has no sign-in and makes no requests outside its own site.

AI

Omark has no AI or machine learning features. It doesn't use, call or include any AI service or model, whether from a third party, an aggregator or hosted by us. It never sends your data, including data from Google APIs, to an AI or machine learning service, and we don't use your data to train, improve or run AI or machine learning models.

Because your files are plain text (Markdown, CSV and YAML) in your own storage, you can open them in any tool you choose, including an AI assistant you run yourself. That is between you and that tool: Omark doesn't connect to it, send it anything or receive anything from it.

Events whose titles don't start with a project code are read but not stored.

What we do with your Google data

  • We use it only to give you the features you see: working out your hours, writing your files, and colouring your events.
  • We don't sell it.
  • We don't use it or transfer it for ads, for credit checks, or to data brokers.
  • We don't use it to train, improve or run AI or machine learning models, and we never send it to an AI service (see AI).
  • No person reads it, unless you explicitly agree, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.
  • We transfer it only to the storage you picked (your Google Drive, OneDrive or a folder on your device), and nowhere else.

You can remove Omark's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Omark's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of data from Google Workspace APIs (Drive and Calendar) also follows the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy.

Data protection

Andrzej Lipka is the controller for the little personal data our sites handle: IP addresses and request data that pass through Cloudflare, and the sign-in cookies.

We handle it to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and to keep the sites secure and free of abuse, which is our legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

The sign-in cookies last as long as the table above shows. Omark keeps no request logs of its own.

Cloudflare may process request data outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards in its data processing terms.

Your rights

Under data protection law (GDPR) you have the right to see, correct and delete your personal data. We hold no data about you on our servers, so there is nothing for us to show or delete there. Your files are in your own storage, where you can open, change or delete them at any time.

If you have a question about your data, write to hello@omark.app. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Poland that is the UODO (Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych).
