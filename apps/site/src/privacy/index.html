Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

In short

  • We don't store your data on our servers. Omark Tasks and Omark Time run in your browser and talk directly to the storage you pick: your OneDrive, your Google Drive, or a folder on your device. Omark Time also reads your own Google Calendar.
  • The apps set one sign-in cookie, so you stay signed in. This site sets no cookies.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party scripts.
  • We ask Microsoft and Google only for the access each feature needs, and only when you use that feature.

Who we are

Omark is run by Andrzej Lipka, an individual in Poland. "We" and "us" on this page mean him. You can reach him at hello@omark.app.

This policy covers the copies of Omark that we run on omark.app and its subdomains: this site, tasks.omark.app, demo-tasks.omark.app, time.omark.app, demo-time.omark.app and the staging-* test sites. Omark is open source, so anyone can run their own copy. Those copies are run by whoever hosts them, and this policy doesn't cover them.

Where your data lives

Your tasks, notes, hours and settings are plain files in the storage you pick. The apps read and write them from your browser. No Omark server receives or keeps a copy.

Signing in

To use OneDrive, Google Drive or Google Calendar, you sign in with your Microsoft or Google account. We never see your password: you type it on Microsoft's or Google's own page.

A small sign-in helper on our host, at /api/auth/*, finishes the sign-in. It swaps the one-time sign-in code for tokens (OAuth with PKCE). It encrypts the long-lived refresh token and puts it in a sign-in cookie in your browser. The cookie is HttpOnly and Secure, so scripts on the page can't read it and it is only sent over HTTPS. When the app needs to reach your storage, the helper uses the cookie to give your browser a short-lived access token. The helper keeps nothing: there is no database and no copy of your tokens on our side.

Signing out clears the cookie.

Cookies

This site, omark.app, sets no cookies. The apps set only the sign-in cookie described above. It is strictly necessary for signing in, so there is no cookie banner. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts.

On your device

So the apps work offline, they keep a copy of your files (in your browser's IndexedDB) and a few settings on your device. This copy stays in your browser. Signing out, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes it.

The demo

The demo at demo-tasks.omark.app (and later demo-time.omark.app) uses sample data kept only in your browser. It has no sign-in and makes no requests outside its own site.

Hosting

Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages) serves our sites and runs the sign-in helper. Like any host, Cloudflare processes IP addresses and request details to deliver pages and to protect the sites from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What we do with your Google data

  • We use it only to give you the features you see: working out your hours, writing your files, and colouring your events.
  • We don't sell it.
  • We don't use it or transfer it for ads, for credit checks, or to data brokers.
  • We don't use it to train AI or machine learning models.
  • No person reads it, unless you explicitly agree, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.
  • We transfer it only to the storage you picked.

You can remove Omark's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Omark Tasks' and Omark Time's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

Under data protection law (GDPR) you have the right to see, correct and delete your personal data. We hold no data about you on our servers, so there is nothing for us to show or delete there. Your files are in your own storage, where you can open, change or delete them at any time.

If you have a question about your data, write to hello@omark.app. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Poland that is the UODO (Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych).
